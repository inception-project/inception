[[sect_settings_agreement]]
= Agreement Settings

After an agreement calculation, {product-name} shows notes on characteristics of the data that
affect how the score should be read. What these notes mean and how they are determined is described
in the <<user-guide.adoc#sect_agreement_notes, User Guide>>. The settings below control when such a
note is considered worth showing.

The defaults are chosen to stay quiet on well-behaved data, on the grounds that a note appearing on
every result is quickly ignored. Projects that want to be told earlier - or later - can move the
thresholds.

NOTE: These thresholds are conventions, not derived constants. Where the line falls between "worth
      mentioning" and "noise" depends on the annotation project, so treat the defaults as a starting
      point rather than as recommended values.

Thresholds expressed as shares must lie between `0` and `1`, limits must not be negative, and
`minimum-basis-for-share` must be at least `1`. An out-of-range value prevents {product-name} from
starting and names the offending setting.

.Agreement diagnostics settings
[cols="4*", options="header"]
|===
| Setting
| Description
| Default
| Example

| `agreement.diagnostics.skew-threshold`
| Share of the assigned labels that the most frequent label must reach (or exceed) for the
  distribution to be reported as skewed. If several labels are equally frequent at the top - which
  can only reach the threshold if it is set to `0.5` or lower - all of them are named.
| `0.85`
| `0.9`

| `agreement.diagnostics.marginal-asymmetry-threshold`
| Difference in label usage between two annotators, as a share of their annotations, at or above
  which their marginals are reported as asymmetric.
| `0.2`
| `0.3`

| `agreement.diagnostics.sparse-category-limit`
| Number of items below which a label is reported as too sparse.
| `5`
| `10`

| `agreement.diagnostics.few-items-limit`
| Number of items that count towards the score below which the score is reported as statistically
  fragile.
| `30`
| `50`

| `agreement.diagnostics.boundary-disagreement-threshold`
| Share of the relevant positions missing from at least one annotator at or above which a
  substantial part of the disagreement is reported as being about boundaries.
| `0.2`
| `0.5`

| `agreement.diagnostics.unusable-document-threshold`
| Share of the documents both annotators of a pair worked on that must fail to produce a score for
  that to be reported (reached or exceeded). At least two documents must be affected;
  `minimum-basis-for-share` does not apply.
| `0.3`
| `0.5`

| `agreement.diagnostics.affected-document-threshold`
| Share of the scored documents that must show a single label, a dominant label (see
  `skew-threshold`) or annotators using labels at different rates (see
  `marginal-asymmetry-threshold`) on their own for these documents to be reported (reached or
  exceeded). Only applies if at least two documents produced a score. `minimum-basis-for-share`
  does not apply to the share of documents, but it does apply within each document.
| `0.25`
| `0.5`

| `agreement.diagnostics.minimum-basis-for-share`
| Smallest number of observations that a share may be computed over before a share-based note is
  reported. It never appears in the text shown to the user. Raising this suppresses notes on small
  studies; lowering it makes them appear sooner.
| `20`
| `50`
|===
